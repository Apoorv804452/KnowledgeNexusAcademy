Medical Entrance

NEET Ultimate Guide

Complete roadmap to crack the National Eligibility cum Entrance Test with Knowledge Nexus Academy.

Overview

NEET (National Eligibility cum Entrance Test) is India's single largest medical entrance examination, conducted by NTA for admission to MBBS, BDS, BAMS, BHMS, and other medical courses. With over 1.5 million aspirants appearing every year, NEET demands strategic preparation, conceptual clarity, and consistent practice.

At Knowledge Nexus Academy, we provide a comprehensive NEET preparation program designed by experienced medical faculty, covering the entire syllabus with regular tests, doubt sessions, and personalized mentorship.

Exam At a Glance

📋 Exam Mode

Online (Computer-Based Test) – Pen & Paper mode also available

⏱️ Duration

3 hours 20 minutes (200 minutes)

📝 Total Questions

200 (180 to be attempted)

🏷️ Marking Scheme

+4 for correct, -1 for incorrect

📚 Subjects

Physics, Chemistry, Biology (Botany & Zoology)

🎯 Total Marks

720 (180 questions × 4 marks)

Detailed Syllabus

The NEET syllabus is based on NCERT curriculum for Class 11 & 12. Here's the subject-wise breakdown:

Physics

  • Class 11: Mechanics, Gravitation, Waves, Thermodynamics
  • Class 12: Electrostatics, Magnetism, Optics, Modern Physics
  • Key topics: Kinematics, Laws of Motion, Work & Energy
  • Rotational Motion, Properties of Matter
  • Current Electricity, EM Waves, Dual Nature

Chemistry

  • Physical: Atomic Structure, Chemical Bonding, Thermodynamics
  • Organic: Basic Principles, Hydrocarbons, Polymers
  • Inorganic: Periodic Table, Coordination Compounds
  • Equilibrium, Redox, Electrochemistry
  • Biomolecules, Chemistry in Everyday Life

Biology

  • Botany: Plant Physiology, Anatomy, Reproduction
  • Zoology: Human Physiology, Animal Kingdom, Genetics
  • Ecology, Evolution, Biotechnology
  • Cell Structure, Biomolecules, Enzymes
  • Human Health, Immunology, Microbes

Eligibility Criteria

  • Age: Minimum 17 years as of December 31 of the admission year. Maximum age limit varies (general: 25 years, SC/ST/OBC: 30 years).
  • Qualification: Passed Class 12 (or equivalent) with Physics, Chemistry, Biology/Biotechnology, and English as core subjects.
  • Minimum Marks: General category: 50% (45% for PwD), SC/ST/OBC: 40%.
  • Attempts: General category: 3 attempts, SC/ST/OBC: No limit.

Preparation Tips

  • NCERT is the Bible: Thoroughly read and revise NCERT textbooks.
  • Mock Tests: Take regular full-length mock tests.
  • Analyze Mistakes: Review your test performance.
  • Conceptual Clarity: Focus on understanding concepts.
  • Daily Practice: Solve at least 50 MCQ questions daily.
  • Revision: Create short notes and revise weekly.

NEET FAQs

  • Is NEET compulsory for BDS? Yes.
  • Can I appear in regional language? Yes, 13 languages available.
  • Is there negative marking? Yes, -1 for each wrong.
  • Can I use a calculator? No.
  • How many attempts? General: 3, SC/ST/OBC: unlimited.

Central Universities

CUET Ultimate Guide

Complete roadmap to crack the Common University Entrance Test for admission to central universities.

Overview

CUET (Common University Entrance Test) is a national-level entrance exam for admission to undergraduate, postgraduate, and PhD programs in 45+ central universities across India. The exam tests language, domain-specific knowledge, and general aptitude.

Our comprehensive CUET preparation program covers all sections with expert faculty, personalized doubt sessions, and regular mock tests to help you secure a top rank.

Exam At a Glance

📋 Exam Mode

Online (Computer-Based Test)

⏱️ Duration

3 hours 15 minutes (total)

📝 Sections

Language, Domain, General Test

🏷️ Marking

+5 for correct, -1 for incorrect

📚 Subjects

As per choice (up to 6)

🎯 Total Marks

Varies by section (max ~800)

Syllabus & Sections

Section 1 – Language

  • Reading Comprehension
  • Verbal Ability
  • Grammar & Vocabulary
  • Sentence Correction
  • Para Jumbles

Section 2 – Domain

  • Choose subjects (Physics, Chem, Math, Bio, Commerce, Humanities etc.)
  • As per Class 11-12 NCERT syllabus
  • Subject-specific MCQs
  • In-depth conceptual questions

Section 3 – General Test

  • Quantitative Reasoning
  • Logical Reasoning
  • Data Interpretation
  • General Knowledge & Current Affairs
  • Analytical Skills

Eligibility

  • Age: No upper age limit for CUET (as per UGC guidelines).
  • Qualification: Passed Class 12 (or equivalent) from a recognized board.
  • Minimum Marks: Varies by university – generally 50% (general) and 45% (SC/ST/OBC).
  • Subject Choice: As per the course you want to pursue.

Preparation Tips

  • Know your subjects: Choose domain subjects wisely.
  • Practice mock tests: Time management is key.
  • Strong vocabulary: For language and general test.
  • Revise NCERT: For domain subjects, NCERT is essential.
